## 29. Community follow-up

### 29.1 Boss overtime and Raid revival

- The maintainer requested the extra Final Assault / Hidden Core overtime grace only for teams with an AI teammate.
  At fight start, a non-departed AI seat preserves the configured 150 real seconds; otherwise the threshold is the
  visible level countdown (120 real seconds). The decision stays fixed during that fight. The first deduction is at
  the following whole real second, at one team LP per real second. `overtimeAt` uses that same threshold.
- Ordinary Raid polls keep the 0.2.3 order: own field first, then remaining route distance and stable id, and they
  still jump only when the skill is ready or the member has been idle long enough and the range is empty.
  The maintainer confirmed one reselect immediately after a revive. That reselect alone orders by taunt, then
  remaining route distance, then id, and it jumps while the operator is not blocking, even if another enemy is
  already in range. Standing on an enemy's tile is blocking: the 0.25 s poll runs before that tick's block pass,
  so the reselect refreshes contact before it decides to stay. A retreat, a move, a redeploy that waited out its
  timer, the initial deployment and a Raid jump do not arm it. [ASSUMED] A revive is recognised as a knock-out
  redeploy that returns before `respawnAt`; the bond text does not name that timer split.
- Regression: `boss-ai-grace.test.js` covers both final fights with/without AI; `community-round29.test.js` covers
  Texas S2 with local enemies, remote taunt/route priority, blocked revival and no repeated self-trigger.

### 29.4 Clementia S2 and final-boss erosion

[PRTS 克莱门莎](https://prts.wiki/w/克莱门莎), checked 2026-10-10, now specifies S2 ordinary-attack splash: radius
1.1 with collision testing, at most four extra ground targets, each taking 100% ATK physical damage. This replaces
the launch-day assumed 1.5 centre radius and copied main-hit multiplier. The primary target retains the skill's
own attack multiplier; the vortex remains radius 1.5. Normal/elite numeric, radius and large-body regressions live in
`op_clemnt.test.js`. The unresolved launch-day carriage/bomb timing assumptions remain documented in §28.1.

The [PRTS mode record's tips](https://prts.wiki/w/卫戍协议：盟约/PRTS盟约记录) explicitly state that Final Assault
leaders do not receive erosion injury. The existing mode-specific immunity remains. All ten final-leader definitions
were compared against all five injury types: erosion was blocked, the other four could burst. Main-game constants
give leaders a 2,000 erosion threshold, so main-game boss identity alone is not general erosion immunity; that does
not override the mode tip or prove every scripted boss phase susceptible. Pith's current three named injury types
are unchanged. No unannounced future-season behavior is inferred.

- This explicit decision supersedes the old blanket shared counts and Muelsyse override. Private DIY stock keeps its
  per-player 8/5 capacities; the video's multiplier describes the shared pool. (Superseded in 0.3.0: co-op doubles
  the private 自选 stock too, 16 / 10 — the maintainer's decision of 2026-10-15, GitHub #650, §32.14.31.)
- Normal/elite operators occupy 1/3 copies by every gain route. Kazemaru still merges two normals, but the elite
  occupies three. Promotion also occupies the full three. Grants beyond capacity keep a negative internal balance:
  selling one copy cannot expose stock while ownership remains above the cap. Public availability and sampling are
  clamped at zero; `raw balance + owned copies = capacity` remains invariant.
- Mimic samples eligible identities rather than remaining copies and can exceed stock, including the third-copy
  branch. This supersedes #207's old empty-pool failure. Other pool-weighted grants keep their selection rules.
- Equipment across every player's hand, temporary area and equipped slots is counted live: normal 1, upgraded 2.
  Shop/choice displays reserve nothing. Exhausted purchases fail before charging or marking the offer taken; draft
  duplicates become unavailable when an earlier choice empties stock, and automatic picks skip them. Equipment
  returned from a sold/merged operator still occupies stock in the hand. 整备 upgrades the bought item only when
  another copy is still free (the purchase already took one, and a golden takes two). Otherwise the item stays
  normal and the 整备 charge is not spent. An uncapped item may still be upgraded. Held copies of every capped
  shop item stay within that cap.
- [ASSUMED] Consumption/destruction/removal by elimination ends ownership and therefore releases equipment stock;
  the video does not separately document every removal path. A third lifetime purchase is possible after a prior
  copy leaves ownership. Non-shop effect-only equipment has no shared shop cap, consistent with unlimited special
  gifts. This never becomes a two-appearance or two-purchase limit for commercial packaging.
- Stock counts alone do not establish probabilities. Existing shop tier shares, uniform item selection and explicit
  effect-pool weights remain, filtered for availability. No new probability curve is inferred from the table.
- `shared-stock.test.js` covers exceptions, shared ownership, stale offers, reacquisition after destruction, upgraded
  occupancy, consumption, duplicate draft exhaustion and Mimic overdraw/release.

### 29.9 Ember proposal (#465) — current behavior preserved

The history in §22.8 and the 0.1.2 changelog records the chosen compromise for queued 深池逐火 enemies. Comparing
the current code with the proposed phase-order swap reproduces that risk: after a blocker takes the next warrior,
the three husk types can be finished by ranged hits in the current reveal window; the proposal hides them throughout
that window and they stand up again. Reveal or spare blocking capacity defeats them in either version. This is not
absolute invulnerability, but it removes the previously chosen counterplay window. The proposal is not adopted;
the existing phase-order assumption remains pending a better official comparison.

### 29.13 Multiplayer boss Raid landing (#475)

The maintainer approved a narrow revision to §26.1 on 2026-10-10 after the report of three remaining multiplayer
seats leaving one boss field unpaired. Only Raid in `mode_multi_*` Final Assault / Hidden Core battles may use the
empty half's normal deployment area (rows 2–5, cols 2–18). This is an explicit maintainer decision on the community
report, not a claim of independently verified official footage. Hand row 0, temporary row 1, single-player mode's
empty boss half and lone-helper 联防's empty half stay closed. Terrain, field bounds, living/downed reservations,
target ordering and attack reach retain their checks. The exception is local to Raid; general board membership,
Ulpian S3 movement, summons and preparation placement do not expand.

`feedback5-raid-own-board.test.js` reproduces the eight left/right, melee/ranged, boss/hidden blocked cases before
the fix, and checks actual solo modes, hand/temp/side margins, terrain and living/downed reservations afterward.
All 287 golden scenarios were regenerated and verified with no additional digest changes. A fresh 80-match sample
also had zero errors and unchanged wins, completed rounds and final LP versus the preceding candidate. Two co-op
HARD runs changed round-14 counters: seed 33's unpaired player killed 22 enemies instead of 26; seed 39's unpaired
player killed 5 instead of 6 and leaked 1 instead of 0. Opening more landing tiles does not guarantee better outcomes.

### 29.14 Device cards after field refresh

Re-entering the same battle with metadata that omits once-spawned devices now restores the remembered device records
to the renderer as well as to the detail-card lookup. Otherwise an HP snapshot recreated the crate as an unknown
enemy: picking emitted a unit click with no definition, instead of the tile click needed by the device card. The
engine-canvas regression retains the card/100 HP assertions and also covers another field refresh, live HP loss and
closing the card when the crate is destroyed. Device metadata supplied by the new entry still wins; changing fields
does not carry old devices across. Gameplay and allied targeting rules are unchanged.
