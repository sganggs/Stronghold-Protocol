### 37.3 COST／SP 飘字（#657）

来源：[issue #657](https://github.com/sganggs/Stronghold-Protocol/issues/657) 正文截图及维护者对范围的回复。
已查看扣费截图 `91695d1f-5010-420f-8a67-838dfa366e7a`（红色 COST −3）及技力截图
`3e5a41e1-a46d-43f4-bd8b-447da3323d9f`（青色 SP 3）。白色正 COST、青色 SP、红色负 COST
位于干员上方。字号、色值、0.9 秒寿命、居中位置及小数最多两位是 [ASSUMED]，未逐像素或逐帧复刻。

按维护者指定的 [ASSUMED] 展示范围：官方数字模式保留情报官技能、冲锋手击杀回费与行商实际扣费；
非官方模式额外显示尖兵技能回费。德克萨斯开场费用、自然费用及忍冬天赋的自然回复增量不显示。
SP 仅 `bond`／`talent`／`skill` 额外回复（含迅捷、萨尔贡），排除自然、普攻、受击、初始技力、
退款及未列来源。两类都受「显示伤害数字」总开关控制，Pixi 与简化棋盘同一来源过滤。

没有把既有 `spGain` 请求或 `fx('sp'/'dp')` 估值作为数字：`gainSp` 在 hook、充能转换、上限之后
计算实际 SP 增量；`addDp` 可选来源参数记录实际 DP 变化，原返回值不变。零到账、阻回、技能期间
拒绝的回复不显示。费用来源的 kit 调用仅补上来源，不修改金额或时序，原特效仍保留且不重复出数字。
`resourceNumbers` 为仅显示用的 Battle 构造选项，默认关；浏览器主线程／Worker 和服务器直播的
战斗入口打开，预演／验证／golden 不打开，因此 golden 的既有事件计数和全部结果字节不变。
事件 `['resource', id, 'cost'|'sp', actualDelta, reason]` 属于可丢弃的短暂外观事件。
测试覆盖上限、充能跨格、hook 改写／拒绝、阻回、实时来源、两种数字模式及开关、Pixi 标签和回收。
红字判定与 #412 的字体／合并自定义不属于本项，保持不变。

### 37.6 人机让出队友的特化干员、少选鸭爵（#121，round54）

5–6 阶、且与存活队友**开局策略**盟约重叠的共享干员扣 18 分，低阶拉特兰不受影响；选策略时鸭爵也不会因为队友降低权重。

改动（`server/match/bot.js`，均为 [ASSUMED] 的人机取舍，不是官方规则）：

- `teammateBonds`：每名存活队友需要的盟约 = 开局策略的盟约 + 公开盟约栏上的主核心盟约（`mainCoreBond`），减去自己开局
  策略需要的盟约和本模式关闭的盟约。真人与人机一样；多名队友不叠加；淘汰的队友不再计入；单人为空。

  （+10／+14）仍压过这项扣分。
- 选策略：机制会改变队友敌人的策略（鸭爵「神秘顾客」：第 5 回合起自己和队友遭遇的部分敌人换成鸭爵／高普尼克／流泪小子／
  圆仔）在有存活队友时只保留十分之一权重（`MATE_BURDEN` = 0.1，按描述识别）；单人照旧。

### 37.11 Waiting-room ready feedback for every room member (PR #887, @YinqiMargatroid)

- **Reported (2026-10-25):** clicking 准备就绪 in a co-op waiting room is silent for both the sender and teammates;
  it should use the briefing's ready sound for everyone in the room.
- **Cause:** the room sends `room.ready` directly without a sound, while `g.infoReady` uses the local action's
  `ready` success cue. There was no listener for ready transitions in the public waiting-room state.
- **Change:** `installAudio` observes an existing human seat changing from unready to ready in a live, same-room
  waiting-room update and plays `sfx.ui.ready` on each client, including the sender and spectators. Snapshot replay,
  restoration, cancellation, seat replacement/AI additions and match lifecycle changes stay silent. Local audio
  settings and browser unlock remain in effect. Cancelling and readying again retriggers the cue with no time-based
  cooldown. No new network frames, translations, game data or sim changes.
- **Evidence / [ASSUMED]:** this shared-room playback policy follows the reporter's request; no official multiplayer
  recording was independently checked. The sound itself reuses the existing briefing `g.infoReady` manifest key.
- **Regression coverage:** room-state subscription tests cover all listeners, repeat/cancel/re-ready, snapshots,
  reconnect restoration, AI/seat changes and match/solo exclusions. A real-server browser test checks shared playback
  and the briefing sound through actual decoded Web Audio sources.

### 37.18 Summons wear their owner's outfit (PR #876, @Flotiarenor; #875)

- **Seen:** the cosmetics layer of PRs #685 / #686 covers operators only: `tokens[id]` has one `avatar` and one `spine`, so every owner of a summon draws the same model, and a plugin cannot key a summon's appearance by the outfit its owner wears. The official skin table carries that relation.
- **Official:** `.cache/gamedata/excel/skin_table.json` `charSkins[*].tokenSkinMap` is an array of `{ tokenId, tokenSkinId }`: 67 outfits, 82 pairs over 54 tokens in the 2026-10-24 client snapshot (66 outfits, 81 pairs in the 2026-10-18 gamedata cache), e.g. `char_250_phatom@sale#4` → `token_10007_phatom_twin_sale#4`; every `tokenSkinId` is a `charSkins` record of its own with an `avatarId`. Summon skins are display art; no battle rule reads an outfit. The official autochess mode's own use of outfits is not established [ASSUMED: the remake's cosmetic layer is the maintainer's decision].
- **Cause:** the overlay's token record had no `skins` map, the server resolved a summon's `skin` through its token record (which has no `charId`, so `skinFor` returned null), and `UnitInfo` carried no owner operator for a client to re-read.
- **Now:** `tokens[id].skins[<owner skinId>]` carries the same skin record an operator's outfit does (pictures and Front/Back model, at most 64, `skinKey` rules; docs/PLUGINS.md §9, `schemas/overlay.schema.json` `$defs.tokenRecord`). The prep board, hand and 临时整备区 resolve the summon through its owner piece (`tokenSkin`), the sim copies the owner unit's `skin` onto the summon at creation and at the late owner link, and `UnitInfo.ownerCharId` lets a running battle re-read the live public choice like an operator body (`skinOf`). `spineEntry` / `tokenAvatarUrl` / `unitPictureUrl` take `opts.skin` in the tokens branch and fall back to the summon's own model and avatar. The picker and `room.skins` are unchanged — a summon has no row of its own — and 「显示其他玩家的皮肤」 applies to a summon as to its owner. Display only: `Unit.skin` is never read by a rule, and the same seeded battle with and without it returns the same `Battle.result()`.
- **[ASSUMED]:** a summon whose owner is not a chess piece (a map character, a stage token) keeps its own model; a summon's outfit is not a separate choice for the player. A mid-battle change reaches a summon through the same public re-read as an operator, not by restarting the battle.
- **Tests:** `test/skins.test.js` (owner-outfit resolution on board / hand / scout / battle, the late owner link, `ownerCharId`, the picture-only fallback, the unchanged result), `test/plugins/schema.test.js` (a summon with owner-skin appearances, and a bad key refused by both the checks and the schema), and the sim guard now names `server/sim/battle/summons.js`. Golden unchanged.

- **Follow-up:** `tokenSkin` explicitly passes a 自选 owner's selected `charId` to `skinFor`, matching `round.js`. A real DIY seat regression covers board, hand, temp, scouting, battle and clearing the choice. The released 0.3.2 history is restored byte for byte.

### 37.30 手动席位不再被内置人机接手（round54，维护者）

维护者指出：Claude 单人手动决策基本在二十秒内，若解决人机抢控制权，就能派代理测多人。原来合作手动席位与其他外部席位一样，
每个决策只有剩余阶段的一半，超时或连续失败后由内置人机接着下（公共机变第二位起每人 16 秒，一半只剩 8 秒），代理的
后续操作被人机覆盖。现在 `manualWait` 的有时限窗口（合作、限时的历史单人版本）：
- 决策可用剩余的整个阶段或回合（仍受 300000 ms 上限），剩余为 0 时也不交给人机；
- 超时后不走人机回退，由阶段倒计时按真人规则处理（休整以当前阵容准备、选策略取高亮或默认、公共机变随机、报价按
  超时规则），下次观察显示 `TIMEOUT`、`fallback: false`；
- 回复无效时在窗口内重新发观察并附 `EXTERNAL_ERROR`，连续至多 `failureLimit` 次，不禁用该席位；累计 256 个意图用完同样交给倒计时。
非手动的外部席位（LLM 顾问等）不变。测试：`test/match/manual-ai.test.js`（合作选策略超时取高亮策略、无回退计数；
沉默的合作席位经过休整倒计时后没有任何人机购买或摆放；无效回复重新询问）。

### 37.47 人机每回合预演 6 种站位（原来 3 种）（round57，Claude）

人机每次准备会用真实战斗把几种候选站位各打一遍，挑漏得最少的（预演，§14）。候选是位置特质的排法和 5 种布阵参数（默认、分散、按住 2 格、
按住 8 格、先杀 3 个），去重后一般 4–6 种，但 `botRehearsal` 默认只试前 3 种。

终极单人 30 局、419 次预演里每次试满 8 种（实际最多 6 种不同的）：胜出的站位有 16% 排在第 3 种之后，「按住 8 格」和「先杀 3 个」合计赢了 12%，
所以调换顺序拿不到这部分。只在最终攻势多试（8 种）完全没变化（单人 1–160 −0.01 ± 0.52），提升都在第 1–13 回合：少漏怪，进最终攻势时生命值
和阵容都更好。

**改动** [ASSUMED]：`BOT_REHEARSAL_DEFAULT` 3 → 6（试 8 种和 6 种逐局相同）。

### 37.48 等待室参赛博士转入观战席（关联 #76）

扩展既有观战席：开局前，参赛博士可通过 `room.spectate` 原子转入空闲观战席，无需离开再加入；房间必须保留另一名真人。
创建者按原规则移交给仍入座的真人（在线优先），新创建者显示「待命中」。成员变动取消尚未结束的版本切换投票。
`room.join` 可重新入座，保留会话中的调配、持有、自选及皮肤设置，重新准备并确认内容；不增加观战席，不允许对局中的参赛者转观战。
界面沿用观战席条和按钮，转入前确认，创建者额外获知身份移交。

验证：`test/lobby-spectate-switch.test.js`、`test/lobby-versions.test.js`、`test/lobby.test.js` 及
`test/ui/room-spectate-switch.e2e.test.js`。这是既有非官方观战功能的便利扩展，不修改模拟、奖励或黄金对局结果。
