## 27. After the 0.2.1 release (unreleased)

The changes merged after the 0.2.1 release (2026-10-07), recorded here until the next release names its version (the
file name 0.2.2 is that version's working name [ASSUMED]). Each rule was checked against the official data and PRTS;
anything no source backs is marked [ASSUMED]. Where each is handled: the co-op room option 「AI 队友最后选择」 → §27.1.

### 27.1 The co-op room option 「AI 队友最后选择」: humans draft before the AI teammates (GitHub #338) — `match/match/phases.js humansFirst / skipBand`, `match/match/spDraft.js enterSpDraft`, `lobby.js setAiPicksLast`, `screens/room.js AiLastToggle`

- **Seen**: in 同盟模拟 the strategy draft (BAND_DRAFT) and the 机变 draft (SP_DRAFT) go round in a random order, and
  what one seat takes is gone for the others (队友已选; the 机变 cards are one shared set). In a room of one human with
  AI teammates the AI seats often pick first and take the strategy or the card the human wanted — e.g. 人事部文档
  (deploy cap 9) or 变形同构体 (GitHub #338).
- **Official**: the official data and PRTS describe the draft order only as random; the remake's AI seats (§6.6) are
  not covered by any source, so their place in the order is a remake option [ASSUMED]. The default keeps the random
  order.
- **Now**: a host setting of a co-op room, off by default: `room.setAiPicksLast {on}` (host only, before the match —
  `ROOM_STARTED` during it, `BAD_TARGET` in a solo room; a change un-readies the other humans like a difficulty change),
  shown to every seat as `room.state.aiPicksLast`, kept across the room's matches and a host migration, handed to the
  match as `opts.aiPicksLast` (only `true` turns it on; ignored in solo). With it on, `Match.humansFirst` puts every human
  seat before every AI seat in the strategy draft's order and in every 机变 draft's order. It is a stable partition
  applied **after** the usual shuffle: the humans keep the order the draft drew among themselves, the AI seats theirs, and
  no random number is drawn — the option off is exactly the old order, and with it on every random stream is, when each
  draft starts, where it would be with it off (the same 机变 cards, the same shop, bot and wave streams); what follows
  can differ only through the picks themselves (a seat that picks earlier may take another strategy or card). A human
  is any seat that is not an AI seat added with room.addBot: a human under AI 托管, disconnected or departed is still
  ordered as a human. All-human and all-AI matches are unchanged.
- **Skip**: without the option a skip moves the player to the end of the strategy draft's order (§6.1). With it on a
  skipping human goes behind the other humans still to pick and ahead of the AI seats; when no other human is left to
  pass to, it goes to the very end as without the option (the player chose to wait) [ASSUMED — the option's intent,
  humans before AI, kept through a skip]. AI seats never skip. 机变 drafts have no skip.
- **Client**: the room screen shows the switch under the difficulty picker in co-op rooms (`aiLastOption`: hidden in
  solo, the host toggles it, the others see it read-only with 「由创建者设置」); the texts go through `t()`.
- Golden: unchanged (the option is off in every scenario; `tools/golden.mjs` all match). Tests:
  `test/match/ai-picks-last.test.js` (the option's default and solo; both drafts off = the old order, on = the
  humans-first partition of the same shuffle with every random stream unchanged; 1 human + 3 AI first in every 机变
  round; 2 humans + 2 AI on interleaved seats; all humans / all AI; AI 托管, disconnected and departed humans; the skip),
  `test/lobby-ai-last.test.js` (protocol validation, host-only, solo refused, `ROOM_STARTED`, `opts.aiPicksLast`, kept
  after the match and a host migration), `test/ui/ai-picks-last.test.js` (the switch and its translations). Docs:
  design/match.md (§6.1 flow), design/network.md (§8.1 messages), design/client.md (§10 room screen), PLAYING.md.
